Drift Survivors

Top-down car combat • Survivor roguelike • Steam (2026)

Description

Drift Survivors is a top-down arena survival action game where drifting is your movement skill and your lifeline. Enemies swarm. Your car is your weapon. Keep sliding, keep firing, keep upgrading.

Survivor-style runs with escalating enemy waves, precision drifting for dodges and control, car-mounted weapons like Homing Rockets and Ricochet Guns, and Japanese-inspired neon arenas. Stay alive as the pressure climbs — find a build that clicks and push for a longer run.
